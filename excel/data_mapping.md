# Customer Data Mapping

## Overview

This document describes how customer information from the source dataset is mapped to Salesforce before import.

Accurate field mapping helps maintain data consistency and reduces the risk of import errors.

---

| Source Data | Salesforce Field |
|-------------|------------------|
| Name | Account Name |
| Email | Email |
| Phone | Phone |
| Company | Company |

---

## Purpose

Field mapping helps ensure customer information is imported into the correct Salesforce fields.

Reviewing mappings before import helps prevent:

- Missing customer information
- Incorrect CRM records
- Reporting inconsistencies
- Data quality issues
